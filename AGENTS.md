# Sell Mineral Rights Pennsylvania — Site-Specific Rules

This project has its own Pennsylvania royalty-statement voice and its own Puretec-derived visual system. Never import a reusable mineral-rights voice pack or reuse another site's hero, FAQ, related rail, article ending, CTA, or decorative language.

## Voice lock

Write as a Pennsylvania royalty-statement and county-record desk. Use Marcellus and Utica development, lease royalty clauses, unit declarations, division orders, paid decimals, production-month detail, gathering/compression/transport deductions, fractional heir ownership, and written offer assumptions. Treat Act 13 impact-fee reporting as public context, never as a substitute for a private owner's royalty statement. Never use first-person singular business narration, instant-cash language, highest-offer claims, fabricated testimonials, guaranteed drilling, or promises of future value.

## Visual lock

Preserve Puretec's real display and body fonts, deep blue/bright cyan palette, full-bleed hero, sticky editorial service ledger, horizontal industry carousel, image-and-stat split, resource-card grid, and dense navigation/footer cadence. Slug pages use one wide hero image, a broad readable owner brief, a Pennsylvania deduction-check FAQ, a related-record docket, and a cyan statement-review CTA. Never insert another site's generic rounded card stack.

## Release blockers

- The street address may appear only inside a Google Maps embed query.
- No footer thumbnails, blank images, tiny images, floating media glitches, emoji, or Unicode-arrow decoration.
- Every slug has exactly one image: its hero image.
- No vertical one-character text, squeezed desktop columns, invisible copy, weak contrast, clipped sections, or overlapping header elements.
- Desktop header includes real routes, 412-555-1041, and a clear contact CTA. Dropdowns work by hover and focus without a pointer gap; the mobile menu and every carousel control work.
- Contact forms contain exactly Name, Email, Phone, and Message.
- Every footer contains one Google Maps embed and no other footer media.
- The homepage hero contains an SEO-driven H1.
- Every page retains the Puretec-derived fonts and theme.
- All visible copy is written as a business, never as an individual author using “I.”

Completion requires both machine gates, full desktop/mobile screenshot review, production deployment, and live alias verification.

## Fleet website-taste requirements

- Preserve this project’s own donor theme and the unique business voice already declared above. Never copy another mineral-rights site’s CTA copy or voice.
- Every taxonomy slug page may render exactly one image: its mapped `/ours/` hero. It must use a centered desktop reading measure, donor-colored related navigation, and a strong final contact plus phone CTA.
- Every public contact form has exactly four visitor fields in this order: Name, Email, Phone, Message. Field names are `name`, `email`, `phone`, and `message`.
- Every desktop header shows 412-555-1041 and a clear CTA that lands on `/contact#contact-form`. Consolidate secondary links before allowing overlap; dropdowns must open on hover/focus and keep the trigger-to-menu gap selectable.
- Every page-level footer contains one Google Maps embed and no other footer media. Contact also contains one unlabeled body map. The street address remains metadata/map-query only and never appears as visible page copy.
- Slug-page heroes, FAQ treatments, related navigation, carousel controls, and closing CTAs must use this donor's own homepage typography and composition. Never substitute the shared look of another fleet site or a recolored universal block.
